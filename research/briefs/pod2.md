Scope: L3 memory (DRAM/NAND/HBM) and L5 interconnect & networking (memory-interface chips, PCIe/CXL retimers, optical, Ethernet, scale-up/scale-out fabrics).

Huawei optical interconnect (8,192 processors, 2026), scale-up fabrics/UB-Mesh. Other domestic retimer/switch/optical-module players. Compare vs Nvidia NVLink/NVSwitch, Broadcom, Astera Labs.
- Moats & unit economics: memory oligopoly cyclicality; Montage oligopolistic switching-cost moat.
Investability focus: CXMT (IPO-filed), YMTC (IPO-filed), Montage (SSE:688008 + HK); Entity-List status; valuations with as-of dates. Also seed chip_catalog with memory/interconnect parts.
