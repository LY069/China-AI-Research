The Thesis02

One line, then the map

China is building a parallel, vertically-indigenized AI stack under export-control pressure — one to several years behind the US frontier at almost every layer, but with the direction, coordination and capital to create a distinct investable ecosystem whose value is migrating from consumer internet to hardware, power and embodied AI.

What's constrained
  • Lithography is the binding chokepoint — and not close to resolved.
  • Software (CUDA) is the deepest gap — 3–5+ years.
  • Compute is rationed — ~2.5 GW inference vs. surging demand.
What's working
  • System-level bet — lose at the chip, win at the rack.
  • Power — the one unambiguous structural edge.
  • Capital markets — a "slow-bull" IPO wave is the 2026 catalyst.

Why It's Its Own Universe03

Two doctrines of AI

United States

A race to superintelligence

Frontier labs treat a few months' lead as potentially permanent. Effort concentrates on the frontier model; services & enterprise automation lead adoption.

China

AI as infrastructure

Like electricity or water — to be diffused across the whole economy, with a pronounced tilt to the physical world (manufacturing, robotics) where China already dominates.

This is not rhetoric. It sets where capital flows (systems & diffusion, not only frontier training), what "winning" means (90% economy-wide AI penetration by 2030, ~70% by 2027), and why China tolerates a chip deficit while racing on rack-scale systems, power and embodied AI. Export controls reframed every dependency as a weaponizable vulnerability — so China indigenizes every choke point, manufacturing captive demand for any firm that can climb the curve.

L1 · The Binding Constraint06

Without EUV, everything upstream is rationed

7nm-class yield — SMIC (DUV) vs. TSMC (EUV)

TSMC (EUV)>80%
SMIC N+2~35%
~2.5× cost / wafer~30× litho stepsself-sufficiency ~35%

The indigenous EUV light source measured ~100–150W mid-2025 vs. >250W needed for high-volume manufacturing. Read the "2028 goal" as a lab prototype, not volume parity.

Equipment self-sufficiency is uneven: etch & clean ~55%, deposition only ~25%, EDA the widest gap (the May-2025 US EDA ban cut Empyrean's H1 profit ~92% even as revenue grew). Two moats forming anyway: AMEC's process-knowledge compounding and NAURA's breadth-as-lock-in, both backed by the 50% domestic-equipment mandate.

Sources: WEB-014 (C), WEB-007 (D), WEB-009/011. Domestic pure-plays are Connect/QFII-restricted; NAURA/SMIC units Entity-Listed (Mar-2025).

L4 · L5 · The Central Battleground07

Lose at the chip, win at the rack

Device level

~60% of an H100

Huawei Ascend 910C inference throughput vs. Nvidia H100 (DeepSeek's own benchmark). ~1–2 generations behind.

System level

8,192 NPUs / rack

CloudMatrix / Atlas 950 lash NPUs into one logical machine — 16 PB/s interconnect, competing with GB200 NVL72 at the rack.

The bill

~4× the power

The rack-scale win is paid for in electricity — which is exactly where China has room (see L0).

The deep moat isn't silicon — it's software. CANN / MindSpore vs. CUDA is a 3–5+ year gap, even as Huawei open-sources CANN and DeepSeek/Zhipu demonstrate frontier training without Nvidia.

The demand engine: state-DC domestic-chip rules escalated 50% (Aug-25) → full foreign-accelerator ban (Nov-25) → ≥80%-domestic national grid. Nvidia's China share fell ~95% (2023) → <60% (H2-25) → "~0" (2026 framing) — but reporting is unreconciled (ByteDance reportedly still ordering ~$14bn of Nvidia in 2026).

L0 · The Structural Edge08

Power is the one advantage that is unambiguous

Electricity generation, 2024

China~10,000 TWh
United States~4,600 TWh
+1,840 GW wind+solar (2025)$580bn grid capexPUE ≤1.25 target

The US faces a power ceiling — a forecast ~49 GW data-center delivery shortfall by 2028. China does not, and is routing curtailed renewables (~9% solar / ~8.5% wind) into data centers via East-Data-West-Compute.

The catch: abundant power is necessary, not sufficient. The payoff is gated on domestic silicon closing the compute gap. This is the report's central conditional — an inference (D), not a fact.

Cleanest listed proxy: CATL (~$420bn, foreign-investable). State Grid is unlisted. Sources: WEB-001/005/021/024.

L7 · L8 · Models & Diffusion09

Commoditize intelligence — on purpose

Share of OpenRouter tokens, 2026

Chinese models~55%*
— DeepSeek alone~17%
US labs (3)~30%

*Among self-selecting, cost-sensitive routing users; rose from ~33%. Qwen: 180k+ derivative models — the largest open ecosystem globally.

A vibrant, largely open-weight field (DeepSeek, Qwen, Doubao, Zhipu, Kimi, MiniMax). Behind at the very frontier, but the gap is months on many benchmarks and the open tier is globally competitive.

Strategy: make good-enough intelligence cheap infrastructure — which caps value capture in models and pressures Western labs' pricing power. Consumer adoption is real (Doubao ~100m DAU, 145m at peak; ~602m GenAI users); enterprise monetization is the weak spot.

Montage's A/H premium inverted (H-share from a 44% discount to a premium) — a marker of international conviction. Forward P/Es for the broad set sit ~18–19 (similar to global peers) but off a far lower earnings base: the bull case and the reason multiples are unreliable.

The Investable Basket13

Own it as a diversifier, not growth beta

Construction logic
  • Low correlation to the global AI supply chain — fortunes turn on Chinese policy & domestic substitution.
  • A policy put beneath the market ("slow bull" — Beijing will buy weakness, sell strength).
  • Size for the overlay — Entity-List + OISP screen out much of L1–L4 for US capital.
Where value pools
  • Advanced foundry (SMIC — shared scarce resource)
  • Interconnect (Montage — genuine global incumbent)
  • Advanced packaging / HBM (emerging)
  • Power (CATL / grid — the structural edge)
  • Model & app layers commoditize by design — value pushed down to hardware, up to physical AI.
Scenarios & Risk14

2027 / 2030 paths

Base

Uneven indigenization: foundry/memory/interconnect/power progress, EUV lags. Competitive rack-scale systems & open models; policy-sustained capex. Equities compound off a low base, high dispersion.

Bull

An EUV or HBM breakthrough, or a decisive rack-scale win, closes the compute gap; the power edge converts. Champions re-rate toward global peers — the diversifier becomes an engine.

Bear

EUV stalls, yields stay low, mandate outruns capability; a Taiwan / rare-earth flashpoint or OISP tightening strands foreign capital. Policy-inflated multiples de-rate hard.

Key gating risk: technology failure at EUV / HBM. Most underappreciated practical risk for Western allocators: the Entity-List + OISP overlay stranding US capital even in freely-trading names.

Part II · Demand16

Not all "China-AI" is a bet on China

The defining v2 refinement: demand splits into three buckets — and only one is a China-policy diversifier.

Global-demand-led
  • Montage
  • Innolight · Eoptolink
  • CATL

Prices off the world AI cycle (Nvidia, hyperscalers). Foreign-investable.

Mixed / dual
  • Alibaba
  • SMIC · Hua Hong
  • ACM

Both the global cycle and domestic substitution.

Domestic-substitution
  • Cambricon · Moore Threads · Biren
  • CXMT · YMTC · Kunlunxin

Demand made by the mandate; turns on policy; mostly closed to US capital.

The "diversifying, low-correlation" thesis applies only to the domestic-substitution bucket. The global-demand names are a lever on the world build-out that happens to list in Shanghai/HK.
