# Analyst Pod Memos

One memo per pod (see `../FRAMEWORK.md §4`). Each memo follows:

1. **Scope & layers covered**
2. **Market structure & value chain** — who's who, share, concentration
3. **Capability vs. global frontier** — gap, timeline to parity
4. **Moats & unit economics** — where margin pools, pricing power
5. **Bottlenecks & dependencies** — what constrains the layer
6. **Named companies** — mapped to `database/company_master.csv`
7. **Investability read** — listing status, Entity-List exposure, valuation notes
8. **Key figures** — logged to `database/metrics_timeseries.csv`
9. **Sources** — with confidence grades
